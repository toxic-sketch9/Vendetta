## 🎯 What is Vendetta?

Vendetta is a simple command-line tool that helps you save your Telegram chat history as neat, offline HTML documents. Whether you want to keep a backup of important conversations, archive family chats, or create a searchable record of your messages, Vendetta does the heavy lifting for you.

Think of it as a personal librarian for your Telegram messages. You give it your chat data, and it organizes everything into clean, readable web pages that you can open in any browser—no internet connection needed.

### Step 2: Prepare Your Telegram Data

Before you can export your chats, you need to get your Telegram data. Here's how:

1. Open Telegram on your computer
2. Go to **Settings** (click the three lines in the top-left corner)
3. Click **Advanced**
4. Click **Export Telegram data**
5. Choose what you want to export (messages, photos, videos, etc.)
6. Select **HTML** as the format
7. Click **Export** and wait for it to finish
8. Note where the exported folder is saved (usually in your Downloads folder)

### Step 3: Run Vendetta

Once you have your Telegram data exported:

1. Double-click the Vendetta file you downloaded
2. A black window (command prompt) will open
3. Type the path to your exported Telegram folder (or drag and drop the folder into the window)
4. Press **Enter**
5. Wait for Vendetta to process your files—this might take a few minutes depending on how many chats you have

### Step 4: View Your Exported Chats

After Vendetta finishes:

1. Look in the same folder where you ran Vendetta
2. You'll see a new folder called **"vendetta_output"** (or similar)
3. Open that folder and double-click **index.html**
4. Your browser will open showing all your chats, neatly organized and searchable

## 📖 How to Use Vendetta

Vendetta works through simple text commands. Here are the basic things you can do:

### Export Your Chats

```
vendetta export path/to/telegram_data
```

Replace `path/to/telegram_data` with the actual location of your exported Telegram files.

### Verify Your Exports

```
vendetta verify path/to/vendetta_output
```

This checks that all your files are complete and working correctly.

### Generate an Index

```
vendetta index path/to/vendetta_output
```

This creates a searchable list of all your conversations.

### Get Help

```
vendetta help
```

Shows all available commands and options.

## 🔧 Troubleshooting Tips

### "I can't find the downloaded file"

Check your browser's Downloads folder (usually in your user folder under "Downloads"). If you're using Chrome or Edge, look for the download icon in the top-right corner of your browser.

### "The black window closes immediately"

This usually means there was an error. Try running Vendetta again and take a screenshot of what appears before it closes. That will help you understand what went wrong.

### "My exported chats are missing photos"

Make sure you selected "Photos" and "Videos" in the Telegram export settings. You might need to re-export your data with more options selected.

### "Vendetta says 'file not found'"

Double-check that you typed the correct folder path. Try dragging the folder into the command window instead of typing the path manually.
